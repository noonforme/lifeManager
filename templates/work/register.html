How this estimate works

Normal pay is normal hours × hourly rate. Overtime pay is overtime hours × hourly rate × 1.5.

The characterized deductions are GPM 20%, VSD 12.52%, and PSD 6.98%. Each named output uses ROUND_HALF_UP to two decimal places; total taxes sum the displayed deductions and net subtracts that displayed total from displayed gross.

This is a fixed version-one characterization and not tax advice.
