# Product

## Purpose

LifeOS is a private personal reconciliation system. It helps one person compare intended life with recorded life across Work, Money, and Habits, then make the smallest useful correction.

## User

The product serves its owner. It assumes a single trusted person using a local device, values privacy over sharing, and favours legibility and truthful records over engagement mechanics.

## Reconciliation loop

1. See today's date and each register's current state.
2. Record what happened in the domain that owns it.
3. Review derived totals and signals.
4. Correct errors without destroying history.
5. Return later to a record whose meaning has not drifted.

## Principles

- **Truth before motivation.** Show empty, unavailable, incomplete, and confirmed states honestly.
- **Records before dashboards.** Derived views never become a second source of truth.
- **Local and private by default.** Personal records stay under the owner's control.
- **Calm over addictive.** No streak anxiety, gamified pressure, or artificial urgency.
- **Explicit over magical.** Calculations, dates, states, and recovery paths are explainable.
- **Useful without scripts.** Core reading and mutation workflows remain server-rendered.
- **Small complete slices.** Each shipped workflow is trustworthy end to end.

## Success

Version one succeeds when the owner can reconcile shifts and pay, transactions and monthly movement, and habits and momentum from one coherent daily entry point; can understand every derived value; and can back up or recover the local record without hidden state. Monthly Money movement is not an account balance.

## Privacy

LifeOS does not require an account, cloud synchronization, telemetry, advertising, or third-party analytics. Personal content must not appear in logs, readiness responses, test fixtures, screenshots, or source control.

## Non-goals

LifeOS is not a social network, employer timekeeping system, bank connection, tax filing service, medical system, project-management suite, or universal knowledge platform. It does not optimize for multiple users, public sharing, real-time collaboration, or mobile-native distribution in version one.
